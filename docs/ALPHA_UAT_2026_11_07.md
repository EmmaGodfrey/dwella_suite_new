# Dwella Suite — Alpha tester handbook

Version 1.0 · 7 November 2026 · Existing TEST environment

## Welcome to Dwella

Dwella helps property managers organise properties, units, tenants, leases, rent invoices, recorded payments, documents and repairs. Tenants use their own portal to view their tenancy, check invoices and statements, report payments and follow repairs. WhatsApp provides another way to reach selected functions.

This alpha is for finding mistakes, confusing steps and missing feedback through real use. A failed test is useful evidence. Please describe what happened even when you are unsure whether it is a bug. Do not assume that a feature works because a button exists.

## Where to test

Open https://app.dwella.properties/test/ in your browser. Registration is at https://app.dwella.properties/test/register. This is the existing TEST application with separate test data. There is no separate alpha website.

Look for the TEST banner before entering anything. Your results should include the date, browser, device and the release/build supplied by the organiser.

Online payments, automatic collections and payouts are disabled for this exercise. Do not send money. Manual payment records and payment-proof review use simulated payments only. SMS and the guided product tour are outside this test. The WhatsApp connection uses a prefilled CONNECT message, not a code sent to you.

Automatic reminder/expiry sweeps and scheduled invoice/renewal jobs are paused for the initial exercise. New user-triggered transactional events, manual invoice generation and explicitly confirmed reminders remain available. Scheduled behaviour can be assigned as a separate organiser-supervised test later.

## Getting your account

- Property manager: choose Create account and register yourself with your own permanent email address. Open the verification email, confirm it and sign in. Complete workspace setup before adding portfolio data.
- Tenant: ask your paired manager to invite your own email address. Open the invitation and create your account or sign in as that email. Accept the invitation. Do not create a manager workspace for a tenant-only exercise.
- Organiser: assign the manager/tenant pairs and admin-only checks. Do not distribute administrator credentials to general testers. Use a separate account or browser profile when changing roles.
- Email verification is required for new accounts to enter their workspace. Tenant invitation acceptance proves access to the invited mailbox. Do not forward verification or invitation links to another person.
- Check Spam/Junk if mail is missing. Record the arrival time and which folder it reached. Wait for the resend cooldown before requesting another link. Report an undelivered message as Blocked rather than marking the account flow passed.
- MFA is available in Settings → Security and is required for administrators. Use your own authenticator. Never attach the QR image, setup key, password or current code to a bug report.

## Safe and useful test data

Use your own real email and WhatsApp number for ownership and delivery checks. Send invitations and notices only to consenting participants. Use fictional names, addresses, properties and tenancy facts for the rest. Prefix records with your tester initials, such as ALPHA-JK Lakeview and ALPHA-JK Unit 01.

Prepare one property, two units, two fictional tenants, one active lease and a full-month invoice for K2,500. Use a due date deliberately chosen for the test. Add a small JPG/PNG repair picture and a clearly labelled dummy payment-proof file. Do not upload real identity documents, bank statements or sensitive tenant records.

Complete the fictional tenant's billing address and the required tax/identity fields before requesting a final invoice PDF. Use the organiser's clearly labelled dummy tax data for TEST only. Do not borrow another person's identifiers. Missing TPIN or billing address should produce a clear correction message; it does not prove that WhatsApp delivery is broken.

For manual balance checks, start with a fresh K2,500 invoice and no opening balance or other adjustments. A K1,000 recorded test payment should leave K1,500; another K1,500 should leave zero. Test opening balances separately so the expected total is unambiguous. A payment reversal corrects a record; it does not return actual money.

## Your first complete journey

- Register or accept your tenant invitation, verify your email and sign in.
- Manager: create the workspace, property, unit, tenant and lease. Generate an invoice and open its document. Tenant: open your own invoice and lease.
- Connect WhatsApp from optional Get Started or Settings → Notifications. Enter your number, choose optional notices, open WhatsApp and send the exact prefilled CONNECT message. Return and choose I've sent the message.
- Open the Dwella chat, send MENU and follow the available role-specific buttons. Try viewing an invoice and statement, then report a repair with a test photo.
- Manager and tenant: follow one repair and one simulated payment-proof report through both accounts. Compare the resulting invoice, receipt and statement.
- Record results in the accompanying workbook, including any blockage. The organiser assigns deeper cases; one person need not run the entire catalogue.

## Modules at a glance

- Get Started: workspace and first-property setup, progress checklist, optional WhatsApp connection.
- Dashboard / Home: portfolio or personal tenancy summary, balances and items needing attention.
- Properties: property and unit details, occupancy and related records.
- Tenants: tenant contact and billing details, invitations and tenant history.
- Leases / My lease: tenancy terms, dates, deposits, lease documents and signing.
- Billing / Payments: invoices, manual payment records, receipts, proof reports and balances. Real payment processing is excluded.
- Reports: rent roll, arrears, recorded collections and tenant statements, including filters and exports.
- Maintenance: repair requests, photos, conversation, progress and vendors where available.
- Messages and notifications: in-app updates, operational notices, email delivery and opted-in WhatsApp notices.
- Settings: account details, workspace settings, security and notification preferences.
- Administration: organiser-only workspace, user, access, delivery-status and audit checks.

## How to execute a case

Read the role, prerequisites in the steps and expected result. Use a fresh record when the case says so. Record the actual result, not just a tick. Check persistence by refreshing after a successful save. For paired tests, the manager and tenant should both confirm their side.

Use Not run until you actually execute a case. Pass means every expected outcome occurred. Fail means an observed result differs. Blocked means a dependency prevented execution; name that dependency. Not applicable needs an organiser-agreed reason, such as a module not exposed to your role. A visible feature failing to load is not automatically Not applicable.

P0 cases cover sign-up, access boundaries, the main invoice/repair/WhatsApp journeys and the disabled-money boundary. Run these first. P1 covers normal and failure journeys. P2 covers additional usability and presentation checks. Cases marked Organiser stay with the internal team.

## Email and WhatsApp expectations

Account mail should arrive promptly; operational mail may wait for a dispatch cycle of approximately five minutes. Record actual timing. WhatsApp notices can queue during quiet hours (20:00–07:00 local time); check the recorded status before treating a delayed notice as failed. Provider acceptance alone does not prove arrival: check the receiving inbox or handset.

WhatsApp number verification and optional proactive notices are separate. You may connect without opting into notices. Send MENU to open the role-specific chat. Send CANCEL or BACK to leave a draft, and STOP to withdraw WhatsApp access/consent. Reconnection requires the normal website flow.

Only send a repair photo or payment-proof file at the stage that asks for it. Random attachments should not create a repair or payment report. Create invoice in Dwella opens the website; invoice creation inside the WhatsApp chat is not part of this release. Free-form AI assistance is not an acceptance requirement.

## Reporting a problem

Use this title: [Module] action and observed problem. Include case ID, role, test record reference, device/browser, date/time, numbered reproduction steps, expected result and actual result. Add a cropped screenshot or short recording with private details removed. Include whether it happens every time and whether refreshing changes it.

Severity: Critical = another user's data is visible, unauthorised money movement, or severe data corruption. High = a main journey cannot be completed. Medium = an important feature is wrong with a workaround. Low = wording, spacing or another minor usability issue. Stop the affected scenario and notify the organiser promptly for a Critical issue.

The workbook contains Cases and Bugs sheets plus a Summary. Do not overwrite expected results to make an observed result pass. Retest a fix against the original case and record the new build/date; retain the original failure in the bug log.

## Organiser launch checklist

- Confirm the TEST URL, registration open, direct delivery to intended tester mailboxes and successful verification/invitation delivery.
- Confirm money movement is disabled on the server and unavailable in the interface, while manual records still work.
- Confirm new participants beyond the original operator phones can complete signed CONNECT and use their authorised WhatsApp menus.
- Assign each participant a role, a record prefix and a consenting partner for invitation/repair/proof tests. Keep at least two independent workspaces for isolation tests.
- Provide dummy tax details, sample files, the current build identifier, a feedback channel and a test window. Define who triages issues and how quickly blockers will be answered.
- Check fresh backups, healthy workers, sender quota and delivery failures. Keep bulk campaigns and unrelated historic queued messages out of the exercise. Never reset shared TEST data without warning participants.
- Run one genuine manager sign-up → email verification → tenant invitation → invoice PDF → WhatsApp journey before inviting the wider group. Synthetic browser checks are not a substitute for inbox/handset evidence.

## Completion and retest

Each tester returns the results workbook and a short note: what was easiest, what was confusing, what blocked you, and the three improvements you would prioritise. The organiser checks unresolved P0 cases, all Critical/High bugs, delivery evidence and cross-role results before deciding whether to broaden access. This alpha does not itself authorise a production release.

The case catalogue and companion files cover the current application modules. Payment gateway processing, SMS, the guided tour, native WhatsApp OTP, paid plans/message credits, native chat invoice creation and other deferred roadmap features are excluded. An excluded feature becoming usable unexpectedly should be reported.
